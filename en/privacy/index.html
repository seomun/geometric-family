Geometric Family ("we", "the operator") makes webtoons, videos, mobile games and this website. This policy explains what personal information we process for each of the services below. The Korean version at https://geometricfamily.com/privacy/ prevails if the two differ.

In short
  • 11 casual game apps (Playground, Coloring Book, Little One's Day, Three Family Table, Shape Merge and others): no accounts, and we do not collect names, contacts, photos or location. Progress stays on your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob in some apps (Geometry Expedition shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob that you choose to watch).
  • Geometry Expedition: an anonymous account (no name or email) stored on our server, with progress, purchase records and invite codes (we do not ask for your birthday or age). You can delete your account in the app or request it on the web.
  • Kids apps (Playground, Coloring Book, Little One's Day) use child-directed, non-personalized ads only, and the advertising ID permission is removed.
  • We never sell personal information or share it with third parties for advertising.

2. Information we process

2-1. Game apps (except Geometry Expedition)

  • The apps themselves collect no personal information: no names, contacts, photos, voice, location or usage analytics, and no sign-up or login. No analytics SDK (such as Firebase) is used.
  • Progress (cleared levels, stars, items and their placement, sound settings) is stored only on the device and is erased by "Reset progress" (in the parent menu for kids apps) or uninstalling the app. It is never sent out.
  • Sibling apps from us installed on the same device may share the in-game "house" (item IDs and positions) through an on-device store limited to apps signed with the same key (kids apps with kids apps, general apps with general apps). There is no server or account, and other apps or third parties cannot read it.
  • "Which Shape Are You?" saves a result image to your photo gallery only when you tap "Save image", without requesting storage permission; the app does not send it anywhere.
  • For information processed through ads and payments, see §4.

2-3. Website

  • No sign-up, and no visit-analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) are used.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical logs such as your IP address to run its servers. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Videos load YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com) only when you press play. Webtoon comments (giscus) are posted through GitHub only if you sign in to GitHub.
  • Web demo progress is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and is not sent to us.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and message in order to reply, and delete them one year after handling (or when a dispute ends, if there is one).

3. Children and teenagers

  • Kids apps (Playground, Coloring Book, Little One's Day) are designed for ages 3–6. The apps collect no personal information directly and have no purchases, notifications or external links. Ads appear only as banners below the home and map screens, with child-directed treatment (TFCD), maximum content rating G, non-personalized ads, and the advertising ID (AD_ID) permission removed. There are no ads during play or in the parent menu (behind an addition gate). They follow the Google Play Families Policy.
  • General apps assume users aged 13 or older; they collect no personal information, so there is no age check.
  • The age rating of Geometry Expedition follows the rating displayed in the store, and it is not directed to children. The app does not ask for or store your age. In-app purchases go through the store's billing, and users under 19 must use them together with a parent or guardian.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that we have, we promptly delete that account's data without requiring a legal guardian's consent. A parent or guardian may contact us to check or delete data.

6. Your rights

  • You (or, for children under 14, a legal guardian) may at any time request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of personal information by contacting us. We will tell you the result within 10 days.
  • Delete a Geometry Expedition account: in the app, open the shop screen and tap "Delete account and data". Without the app, see the account deletion page. Deleting removes progress, companion and currency-ledger records, recruit records, the device identifier and request logs. Purchase records, which we must retain by law, remain for the retention period.
  • Other game apps: we hold no information about you, so "Reset progress" or uninstalling the app erases all on-device records.
  • Ad settings: in your device settings you can reset or delete your advertising ID or turn personalized ads off.

7. Destruction procedure and method

Information whose retention period has ended or whose purpose has been fulfilled is destroyed without delay. Server data is erased by an irrecoverable method; information we must retain by law (purchase records) is stored separately from other information and erased when the period ends.

8. Security measures

  • All server traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Server access is limited to one person, the operator.
  • Accounts are random numbers without names or emails, so that a person is hard to identify even if data leaks.
  • Access keys are not kept in public repositories; they are stored only on the operator's device.

9. Geometric Family Uploader (Google API)

"Geometric Family Uploader" is an internal tool the operator uses to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel. It is not distributed to other users and does not ask them to sign in.

  • Access scope: YouTube Data API v3 youtube.upload and youtube — used only to upload videos to our channel, set titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status and thumbnails, add videos to playlists, and check processing status.
  • Storage: the sign-in token is stored only in a private folder on the operator's computer and never sent to any server or third party. We do not read or store any other user's Google data, watch history or subscriber information.
  • Sharing: Google user data is never sold, provided to third parties or used for advertising, and is not read by humans.
  • In the same way, we may use the Instagram (Meta) Graph API only to post Reels from the operator's own account; it processes no other user's information.
  • Geometric Family Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; the Google Privacy Policy also applies. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

10. Automatic collection (cookies etc.)

We do not track you with cookies. The apps and web demo store progress only on the device (browser localStorage). External services such as ad SDKs, YouTube and GitHub may use cookies or identifiers under their own policies, which you can refuse in your browser or device settings.
