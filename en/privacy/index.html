Geometric Family ("we", "the operator") makes webtoons, videos, mobile games and this website. This policy explains what personal information we process for each of the services below. The Korean version at https://geometricfamily.com/privacy/ prevails if the two differ.

In short
  • 11 casual game apps (Playground, Coloring Book, Little One's Day, Three Family Table, Shape Merge and others): no accounts, and we do not collect names, contacts, photos or location. Progress stays on your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob.
  • Geometry Expedition: an anonymous account (no name or email) stored on our server, with birth year-month for age checks, progress and purchase records. You can delete your account in the app or request it on the web.
  • Kids apps (Playground, Coloring Book, Little One's Day) use child-directed, non-personalized ads only, and the advertising ID permission is removed.
  • We never sell personal information or share it with third parties for advertising.

2. Information we process

2-1. Game apps (except Geometry Expedition)

  • The apps themselves collect no personal information: no sign-up, no analytics SDK.
  • Progress (cleared levels, stars, items and their placement, sound settings) is stored only on the device and is erased by "Reset progress" or uninstalling the app.
  • Sibling apps from us installed on the same device may share the in-game "house" (item IDs and positions) through an on-device store limited to apps signed with the same key. Nothing leaves the device.
  • "Which Shape Are You?" saves a result image to your photo gallery only when you tap "Save image", without requesting storage permission.

2-3. Website

No sign-up and no analytics. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub may process technical logs such as IP addresses) and loads Google Fonts. Videos load YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) only when you press play. If you email us, we keep your address and message for one year after we reply.

3. Children

  • Kids apps are designed for ages 3–6 and comply with the Google Play Families Policy: no data collected by the app, no purchases, no notifications, no external links; banner ads only on the home and map screens, with child-directed treatment (TFCD), max ad content rating G, non-personalized ads, and the AD_ID permission removed. Parent areas sit behind a math gate and show no ads.
  • Geometry Expedition is for users aged 15 and over and blocks sign-up below that age, so we do not knowingly process data of children under 14. If we learn that we have, we delete it promptly. Parents can contact us at any time.

5. Your rights

  • You (or a parent or guardian) may request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing by contacting us. We respond within 10 days.
  • Delete a Geometry Expedition account: in the app, open the shop screen and tap "Delete account and data". Without the app, see the account deletion page. Purchase records are kept for the legal retention period.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

6. Security

All server traffic uses HTTPS, server access is limited to the operator, accounts are random IDs without names, and access keys are never stored in public repositories.

7. Cookies

We do not use cookies to track you. Third-party services (AdMob, YouTube, GitHub) may use cookies or identifiers under their own policies.

8. Geometric Family Uploader (Google API Services)

"Geometric Family Uploader" is an internal tool the operator uses to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel. It is not offered to other users.

  • Scopes: YouTube Data API v3 youtube.upload and youtube — used only to upload videos to our channel, set titles, descriptions, tags, privacy status and thumbnails, add videos to playlists, and check processing status.
  • Storage: the OAuth token is stored only in a private folder on the operator's computer and never sent to any server or third party. We do not read or store any other user's Google data, watch history or subscriber information.
  • Sharing: Google user data is never sold, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or read by humans.
  • Geometric Family Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; the Google Privacy Policy also applies. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
